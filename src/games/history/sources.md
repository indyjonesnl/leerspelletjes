# History Timeline sources

Each event in `data.ts` links directly to a supporting institution, museum, archive, or history reference. These links are provided in both the revealed chronology and the Learn First timeline.

Dutch history is grounded primarily in Canon van Nederland, with Rijksmuseum and Rijkswaterstaat references for the VOC and Delta Works. The VOC description includes its colonial and exploitative context; the end of slavery notes the ten-year forced-labour period after legal abolition.

World history references include the Metropolitan Museum of Art, UNESCO, Greece's Ministry of Culture, British Museum, Gutenberg Museum, Library of Congress, Smithsonian, Imperial War Museums, United Nations, and NASA. Paper and the ancient Olympics are presented as traditional or recorded dates where earlier evidence or uncertainty exists. For BCE dates, the data stores astronomical signed years (`1 BCE = 0`, `2 BCE = -1`); rendering converts that convention to BCE/v.Chr. and never displays year zero. Approximate historical dates are marked “c.” / “ca.”
