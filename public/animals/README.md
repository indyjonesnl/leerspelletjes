## Clue references

Clues describe familiar distinguishing traits, without claiming that all
members of a group share every trait or live in only one habitat. The bee
illustration and honey clue represent a honeybee; the shell clue represents
the illustrated shelled snail and turtle. There are no Arctic penguin or
"all penguins live in Antarctica" claims.

- [San Diego Zoo: elephant](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/elephant): trunk and plant food.
- [San Diego Zoo: giraffe](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/giraffe): long neck, coat and browsing.
- [San Diego Zoo: kangaroo and wallaby](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/kangaroo-and-wallaby): hopping, Australia and pouch.
- [San Diego Zoo: turtle and tortoise](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/turtle-and-tortoise): reptiles and protective shell.
- [San Diego Zoo: amphibians](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/amphibians): aquatic tadpole stage and transformation.
- [Monterey Bay Aquarium: Penguin 101](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/about-us/stories/penguin-101): flightless birds and swimming flippers.
- [Monterey Bay Aquarium: giant Pacific octopus](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/giant-pacific-octopus): eight arms and suckers.
- [NOAA: Are dolphins fish?](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/dolphin.html): mammals, breathing air and blowhole.
