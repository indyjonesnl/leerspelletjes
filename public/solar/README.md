# Solar System image sources

Downloaded unchanged from official NASA-hosted 400-pixel thumbnails on 2026-10-13.
No AI generation, recolouring, compositing, or local photo editing was used.
Combined image payload is about 87 KB. NASA images may be processed composites;
colours are not always how a planet would look to the unaided eye. For example,
PIA00343 has enhanced colours, and PIA23791 combines spectral filters.

The eight planets, their Sun-outward order, and Pluto's dwarf-planet status are
verified against https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/.
Additional facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mercury/facts/,
https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/,
https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/,
https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/facts/,

The order diagram deliberately uses equal-sized numbered slots; neither sizes
nor distances are to scale. Ice-giant picture questions include factual clues
because photographs alone are a poor test of their identity.

## Usage basis

NASA's media guidelines allow factual educational use of NASA content with
acknowledgement, without implying NASA endorsement. They also warn that a
third-party image on NASA's website does not automatically carry reuse rights:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/.

NASA/JPL's explicit image-use policy allows educational use of imagery published
on JPL public sites, including credited mission-partner imagery. Its third-party
section allows journalistic, educational and personal uses while reserving
commercial permissions. The MESSENGER and Cassini partner images are used in
this factual educational game under that policy; preserve their full credit
lines. The corresponding JPL catalogue identifiers are PIA15162 and PIA21345.
Policy: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/.

Do not infer permission for unrelated commercial promotion, merchandise, NASA
logos or NASA endorsement from these educational image permissions. No NASA
logos or identifiable people are included in these game assets.
